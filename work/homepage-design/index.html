What it does

A homepage design laid out as one continuous editorial page rather than stacked blocks, with very large display type and three free typefaces.

Why I built it

She needed a homepage that felt like her music rather than a template, covering her performances, music, teaching and bookings.

My role and the AI's role

  • Me: the brief and the direction for each round.
  • Claude: designed and coded each version as a claude.ai artifact.

How it works

I directed Claude through ten versions in a single day. Photo slots were left as clearly marked placeholders instead of stock images, so nothing on the page pretends to be real.

What I caught and changed

The first version was a scrollable preview built from separate blocks. Over the day it became one continuous page, and the final versions reorganised the whole structure around a single theme.

How it was checked

Ten versions were saved in one day. There was no other testing; it's a design prototype.

Where it is now

A design prototype. It hasn't been launched, and the photographs and final copy are still to come.

Tools and credits

  • Built with Claude (Anthropic) as a claude.ai artifact.
  • Three Google Fonts typefaces under open licences.
