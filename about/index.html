About

I'm Aditya Soni. I graduated from UC Santa Barbara in 2026 with a BA in Economics and Accounting, and I'm based in London.

For five years I worked at 64Facets, a fine-jewellery business, across operations, finance and growth. I opened new stockist accounts in Europe and the Middle East, ran paid digital campaigns, did the monthly variance analysis, prepared for audits, and worked trunk shows at Neiman Marcus, Saks Fifth Avenue, Bergdorf Goodman and Nordstrom. That's where I saw how much of a working day goes on re-typing, checking and chasing, and where I started using AI to take it on: a CRM migration largely automated with a local model, then AI-assisted steps across four business processes (details).

Since then I've built AI systems for my own work and life. The hard part is rarely getting AI to do something; it's making sure it does the right thing every time. So everything I build starts with written rules and ends with checks: tests, a second model's review, baselines, and an honest note of what doesn't work yet.

Before that, I rated AI-model answers for accuracy and reasoning as a contractor with Outlier AI, and analysed company financials and credit risk as an intern at Loan Frame.

What I'm looking for

AI automation, AI operations, AI business analyst and AI enablement roles: work where I find the manual processes worth automating, build them with AI, check they're right, and help a team actually use them. I'm not looking for software engineering roles.

I speak English and Hindi.
